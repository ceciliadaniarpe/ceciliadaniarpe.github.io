Cecilia Daniar Pè

PhD Researcher
Department of Sociology, University of Zurich

Ciao! I am a PhD candidate at the Department of Sociology at the University of Zurich.

My research focuses on social inequality in education and the labor market. I am particularly interested in how socioeconomic background shapes individuals' educational and career trajectories, the opportunities they perceive as accessible, and broader patterns of social mobility. In my work, I use both observational and experimental methods.

My research is funded by the University Research Priority Program (URPP) "Equality of Opportunity" , an interdisciplinary research program at the University of Zurich that examines the causes and consequences of social and economic inequalities, as well as policies aimed at fostering greater equality of opportunity.
